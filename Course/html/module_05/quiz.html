Text Overlay — Knowledge Check

📋
Knowledge Check
Answer all questions — 70% required to mark module complete
Question 1 of 5
A FreeText annotation has /MK << /BG [1 1 1] >>. What does /BG [1 1 1] indicate?

/BG sets the annotation's background colour using RGB values 0–1. [1 1 1] is white. An opaque white background means the annotation visually covers any underlying text — the core mechanism of annotation-based overlay.

Question 2 of 5
In a decoded content stream you find: '1 1 1 rg 68 630 120 16 re f'. What is this doing?

'1 1 1 rg' sets fill colour to white RGB. '68 630 120 16 re' defines a rectangle. 'f' fills it white. This covers any content at that location — the white-box overlay technique.

Question 3 of 5
What makes text-over-text overlay the hardest technique to detect in a PDF viewer?

Text-over-text leaves no annotation entry, uses no white masking rectangle, and the replacement character simply paints over the original. Detection requires decoding the content stream and comparing BT block coordinates.

Question 4 of 5
An annotation was created on 20 March 2025, but the document /CreationDate is 10 March 2025. What is the forensic significance?

An annotation /CreationDate later than the document /CreationDate confirms the annotation was added after the document was created. Combined with an opaque background and differing content, this is evidence of post-creation modification.

Question 5 of 5
Which command is most direct for finding the white-box sequence in a content stream?

'qpdf --show-object=N --filtered-stream-data' decodes and outputs the raw content stream operators. You can then search for '1 1 1 rg' (white fill) to locate any white-box overlay sequences.

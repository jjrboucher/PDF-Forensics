Question 1 of 10
You open a PDF and find /Creator = 'Skia/PDF m124'. What does this tell you?

Skia is Google's 2D graphics library. 'Skia/PDF mXXX' in the Producer field identifies Chrome's built-in print-to-PDF function at Chrome milestone XXX. This document was produced by printing a web page or email in Chrome.

Question 2 of 10
A PDF has 3 %%EOF markers. V1 Producer is 'Microsoft Word', V2/V3 Producer is 'Adobe Acrobat Reader'. An annotation changes a salary value. What is the most accurate summary?

The evidence chain: V1 (Word) = original creation. V2/V3 (Acrobat Reader) = subsequent edits. The annotation with opaque white background overlaying the salary is the modification mechanism. The %%EOF count, Producer history, and annotation dates together tell this story.

Question 3 of 10
What is the fastest method to determine whether a PDF contains any text overlay annotations?

The Text Overlay Detection report runs all three overlay detectors and lists every finding with type, page, object reference, and covered/replacement text values. This is the fastest triage method — manual verification follows for confirmed findings.

Question 4 of 10
A BBOX_CROP finding shows Form BBox [0 0 300 200] and image placement '600 0 0 400 0 0 cm'. What fraction of the image is visible?

Image placed at 600x400 pts, BBox clips to 300x200 pts. Visible width = 300/600 = 50%. Visible height = 200/400 = 50%. Visible area = 50% x 50% = 25%. Three quarters (75%) is hidden.

Question 5 of 10
GPS coordinates 25.2048°N, 55.2708°E are found in an image from a document claiming London provenance. What is the correct forensic response?

25.2°N, 55.3°E is Dubai, UAE — not London. Document the specific GPS values, verify the location independently, note the discrepancy, and seek corroborating evidence. Do not accept or reject the claim on GPS alone.

Question 6 of 10
Which command independently verifies a TEXT_OVERLAY annotation finding?

'qpdf --show-object=N' displays the raw dictionary of object N. For an annotation, this reveals /Rect (position), /Contents (text), /MK /BG (background), and /CreationDate — allowing independent verification of every field the application flagged.

Question 7 of 10
An Info dictionary shows ModDate = 15 Feb 2025, CreationDate = 1 Mar 2025, and XMP shows ModifyDate = 2025-03-01. How many anomalies are present?

Anomaly 1: Info ModDate (Feb 15) precedes CreationDate (Mar 1) — impossible. Anomaly 2: Info ModDate (Feb 15) differs from XMP ModifyDate (Mar 1) — mismatch. Two distinct anomalies, both indicating post-creation editing.

Question 8 of 10
Why is text-over-text overlay harder to detect than annotation overlay?

Annotation overlays appear in /Annots and can be listed. White-box overlays leave a '1 1 1 rg re f' sequence. Text-over-text leaves only two BT...ET blocks at the same coordinates — invisible in any viewer and only detectable by analysing the decoded stream.

Question 9 of 10
What must you document when reporting a NEGATIVE finding (no manipulation artifacts)?

A negative finding is as evidentially significant as a positive one. You must document: the file MD5 (proving you analysed the right file), the tool version (for reproducibility), which reports were reviewed (defining scope), and the date.

Question 10 of 10
You are about to testify about a BBOX_CROP finding. What four elements must your testimony include?

BBOX_CROP testimony must include: (1) the /BBox values and image display size (structural evidence); (2) what the extracted uncropped image contains (content evidence); (3) the percentage of image hidden (magnitude); (4) the independent verification method (reproducibility).

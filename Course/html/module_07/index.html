Module 07 — Capstone

Capstone Exercise

A complete, unsupported forensic analysis of a multi-artifact document — culminating in a report and testimony preparation exercise.

⏱ ~90 minutes📄 Lab file: lab07_capstone.pdf⚠️ No hints
🚨
Capstone Rules

Do not read the answer key until you have completed your own analysis. This exercise tests independent examination without guidance. Work through the Quick-Start Checklist: hash → triage → metadata → images → text overlay → EXIF → document findings.

📋 The Scenario

Document: Service Agreement SA-2025-0847 between Northgate Consulting Ltd and Vantage Solutions Inc.

Claimed value: $240,000 over 12 months

Claimed creation: Microsoft Word 2021, 15 October 2025, London

Your task: Conduct a complete forensic analysis. There are 5 distinct findings across 5 pages.

📋 Deliverables

  1. A findings table: finding ID, page, artifact type, description, supporting evidence (object number / command)
  2. A summary of the net financial impact of all identified manipulations
  3. For CAP-02: a two-sentence court-ready statement
  4. The MD5 hash of lab07_capstone.pdf recorded in your notes

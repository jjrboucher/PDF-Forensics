Answer Summary

Five distinct manipulation artifacts across five pages. A complete forensic analysis should report CAP-01 through CAP-05 with supporting evidence from peepdf3/qpdf/mutool and the PDF Forensics application.

📝 Lab Exercises — Questions & Answers

❓
Question 1

List all manipulation findings in this document by type and page.

Answer: CAP-01 (p1): Metadata mismatch; CAP-02 (p2): FreeText annotation overlay; CAP-03 (p3): BBOX_CROP; CAP-04 (p4): White-box text overlay; CAP-05 (p5): EXIF GPS anomaly

❓
Question 2

What is the net financial impact of the manipulations you can identify?

Answer: Page 4: $240,000 → $180,000 (fee reduced by $60,000). Page 3: Annex A crops out original $45,000 payment schedule amount. Page 2: First payment delayed from Nov 2025 to Feb 2026.

❓
Question 3

The photograph on page 5 is claimed to show a London signing ceremony. What evidence contradicts this?

Answer: EXIF GPS = Dubai (25.2°N, 55.3°E). EXIF DateTime = Oct 28, not Oct 15. Device = iPhone 15 Pro, not a document scanner.

❓
Question 4

How would you present CAP-02 (page 2 annotation) in a court exhibit?

Answer: Produce: (1) Screenshot of page 2 in normal view showing altered date; (2) annotation object dump from peepdf3 showing /Rect, /Contents, /CreationDate; (3) screenshot of the Visual Overlay Report before/after toggle; (4) extracted version 1 of the PDF showing the original date.

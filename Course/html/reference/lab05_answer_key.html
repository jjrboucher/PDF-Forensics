📝 Lab Exercises — Questions & Answers

❓
Question 1

On page 1, what is the object number of the FreeText annotation?

Answer: 7 (verify with: qpdf --json lab05_textoverlay.pdf | grep Annots)

❓
Question 2

On page 1, what date does the annotation display, and what date does the underlying text contain?

Answer: Annotation shows: Wednesday, 19 March 2025. Underlying text: Saturday, 15 March 2025

❓
Question 3

On page 2, write the operator sequence that creates the white-box overlay.

Answer: 1 1 1 rg (set fill colour white) → 68 630 120 16 re (define rectangle) → f (fill)

❓
Question 4

On page 3, how would you confirm the text-over-text manipulation using qpdf?

Answer: qpdf --filtered-stream-data --show-object=<page3_contents_obj> lab05_textoverlay.pdf → look for two BT blocks with identical Td coordinates but different text

❓
Question 5

Which of the three overlay types is hardest to detect visually in a PDF viewer, and why?

Answer: Text-over-text (page 3) — no white rectangle, no annotation visible in the Annotations panel; only the replacement glyph is visible at that position.

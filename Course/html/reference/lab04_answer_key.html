📝 Lab Exercises — Questions & Answers

❓
Question 1

On page 1, what percentage of Image A's content is hidden by the BBox crop?

Answer: 75% — the Form BBox is 200x150 but the image is placed at 400x300, so right 50% and upper 50% are cropped.

❓
Question 2

Extract Image C (the hidden image on page 2) and describe its content.

Answer: Red background image with text 'HIDDEN DOCUMENT'. MD5: 4ebd8cefc75e42300bd071f5b86f249f

❓
Question 3

On page 3, which edge of the image is off-page and by how many points?

Answer: Left edge. Image placed at x=-80, MediaBox left edge is x=0. Off-page by 80 pts.

❓
Question 4

Calculate the effective DPI for Image E on page 4.

Answer: 150 pixels × 72 / 150 pts = 72 DPI

❓
Question 5

What does a 72-DPI image in an otherwise 300-DPI scanned document suggest?

Answer: The image was not scanned — it is likely a screen capture or web graphic inserted into the document.

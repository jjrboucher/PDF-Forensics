📝 Lab Exercises — Questions & Answers

❓
Question 1

Extract Image 2 (page 2) and identify the GPS coordinates.

Answer: GPS: 48.8566°N, 2.3522°E — Paris, France. Use: mutool extract lab06.pdf then exiftool <image_file>

❓
Question 2

What does the EXIF Make/Model on page 2 reveal?

Answer: SAMSUNG SM-G991B — a Samsung Galaxy smartphone. Document claims images are 'scanned' but this is a phone photo.

❓
Question 3

Compare EXIF Make across all three pages. What is the forensic significance?

Answer: Page 1: Canon scanner; Page 2: Samsung smartphone; Page 3: Fujifilm mirrorless camera. Three different capture devices in one supposedly uniformly-scanned document.

❓
Question 4

Using peepdf3, list all annotations and their authors.

Answer: pdf> object <page_obj_num> → check /Annots array → object each annotation → read /T (author) and /Contents

❓
Question 5

What is the timestamp of the M. Patel (QA) highlight annotation on page 2?

Answer: D:20251115104500+00'00' (2025-11-15 10:45:00 UTC)
